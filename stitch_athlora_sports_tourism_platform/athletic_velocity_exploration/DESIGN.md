## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance sports technology, active outdoor adventure, and spatial navigation. Built to convey kinetic momentum, precision, and reliable endurance, the design language bridges the discipline of elite athletic training with the bold spontaneity of geographic discovery.

The style blends **Modern High-Performance Athletics** with **Crisp Functional Tech**:
- **Dynamic Polarity:** Deep midnight navy anchors the interface with authority, while radiant orbital orange ignites key action pathways and interactive touchpoints.
- **Precision Surfaces:** Pure, luminous white and cool-slate surfaces allow data visualization, telemetry, maps, and athletic stats to breathe with optical clarity.
- **Architectural Aerodynamics:** Subtle continuous curves, athletic pill tags, directional indicators, and clean hairline structures mirror high-velocity sport equipment and GPS adventure instrumentation.

## Colors

The palette directly derives from the high-contrast athletic lettermark and dynamic orbital swoop:
- **Primary Navy (`#0A1B3A`):** The structural anchor. Used for primary typography, dominant app headers, heavy display titles, and strong active states.
- **Secondary Radiant Orange (`#FF5E00`):** The kinetic ignition color. Reserved for primary calls to action, active navigation paths, live tracker beacons, compass cues, and metric spikes.
- **Tertiary Trail Blue (`#2563EB`):** A supportive precision blue for informational tags, route overlays, secondary telemetry, and connected device badges.
- **Slate Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool slate spectrum providing clear hierarchy from dark neutral text down to faint border lines (`#E2E8F0`) and light utility backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Strategy:** Built upon pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting on an ultra-clean foundation, preserving crisp separation and outdoor daylight legibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, mirroring the robust geometric geometry and forward-leaning momentum of the brand lettermark. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Utilize Extra Bold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) weights with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project confidence and athletic authority.
- **Body:** Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights ensure effortless reading across metric dashboards, user stats, and route guides.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Set in uppercase Bold (`700` / `800`) with generous letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.06em`), ideal for speed indicators, GPS coordinates, elevation metrics, and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8pt dynamic fluid grid designed for rapid glances during motion or training:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at 1280px to maintain ergonomic field of view.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric cards reflow from 4-column rows into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` safe-edge margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive controls adhere strictly to a 48px minimum hit target for thumb-driven navigation.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight data-to-label couplings (e.g., heart rate numbers and BPM units), `space-md` for component interiors, and `space-xl` for distinct dashboard sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of crisp outlines and athletic ambient shadows:
- **Surface Stacking:** Base canvases rest on `#F8FAFC`, while interactive and content modules elevate onto pure `#FFFFFF` cards bordered with subtle slate `#E2E8F0` hairline strokes (1px).
- **Athletic Shadows:** Shadows feature a crisp, cool-slate tint rather than generic black:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(10, 27, 58, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(10, 27, 58, 0.06)`
  - *Floating / Hover:* `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(10, 27, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 27, 58, 0.04)`
  - *Active CTA Glow:* `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(255, 94, 0, 0.35)` to create an energized halo around primary action buttons.
- **Overlays & Top Navigation:** Backdrops utilize frosted translucent glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `12px` blur) to ensure spatial context remains visible beneath sticky route bars and navigation heads.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for cards) combined with targeted full-pill contours for dynamic elements:
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) delivers modern aerodynamic stability without feeling toy-like.
- **Interactive Badges & CTAs:** Quick-reaction elements—such as route chips, filter tags, status badges, and primary pill buttons—utilize full rounded capsules (`9999px`) echoing the dynamic curvature of the brand swoop.
- **Form Controls:** Inputs and segment controls adopt `0.5rem` (8px) corners for crisp, precise alignment with tabular numbers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Radiant orange background (`#FF5E00`), white bold text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), with `space-md` horizontal padding. On hover, shifts to `#E65500` with an energized ambient glow.
- **Secondary Navy Button:** Deep navy background (`#0A1B3A`), white text, matching pill radius. Used for high-emphasis administrative actions or navigation controls.
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Pure white background, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0A1B3A`. On hover, borders shift to `#0A1B3A` with a soft slate tint.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Status Tags:** Pill-shaped capsules with compact vertical padding (`space-xs`) and horizontal padding (`space-sm`).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Orange-tinted badges (`#FFF7ED` fill with `#FF5E00` text and 1px `#FED7AA` border) for active trails, live sessions, and achievements.
- **Category Chips:** Neutral slate pills (`#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text) that shift to solid `#0A1B3A` when selected.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Input Bars:** Pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` border radius, and inset icon slots (location pins, search optics). Focus states feature a crisp 2px border in `#0A1B3A` with no offset outline blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp 20px targets with 4px border radius for checkboxes and full circles for radios; filled with vibrant orange (`#FF5E00`) upon selection with high-contrast white checkmarks.

### Cards & Stat Widgets
- **Activity & Metric Cards:** Pure white ground, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, `1rem` corner rounding, and resting slate shadow. Top headers pair bold navy statistics with miniature radiant orange trend arrows and geo-compass icons.
- **Hero Adventure Card:** Deep navy background (`#0A1B3A`) with bright orange metric accents, displaying dynamic outdoor maps, altitude graphs, and active waypoint pins.
